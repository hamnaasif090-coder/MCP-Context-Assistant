# Company Onboarding Guide

Welcome to the organization! This guide covers everything you need to know in your first 30 days.

## Week 1: Setup & Orientation

### IT Setup
- Request your laptop from IT within day 1. Submit ticket at helpdesk@company.com
- Set up your company email using your employee ID
- Install required software: Slack, Zoom, VS Code, 1Password
- Enable 2FA on all company accounts — this is mandatory

### HR Paperwork
All new employees must complete the following within 5 business days:
- I-9 Employment Eligibility Verification
- Direct deposit form via the HR portal
- Benefits enrollment (health, dental, vision) — deadline is Day 30
- Emergency contact information

### Access Provisioning
Your manager will submit an access request for:
- GitHub organization membership
- AWS console (if applicable to your role)
- Jira and Confluence
- Internal dashboard

## Week 2: Team Integration

### Meeting Your Team
- Schedule 1:1s with all direct teammates in your first week
- Attend the weekly all-hands every Monday at 10 AM EST
- Join the #introductions Slack channel and post a short bio

### Development Environment
For engineers:
```bash
git clone https://github.com/company/dev-setup
cd dev-setup && ./bootstrap.sh
```

## Company Policies

### Remote Work Policy
Employees may work remotely up to 3 days per week. Core hours are 10 AM – 3 PM in your local timezone. All team meetings must be attended regardless of location.

### Time Off
- 15 days PTO per year (prorated for start date)
- 10 federal holidays
- Unlimited sick days with manager approval
- Submit time off requests via the HR portal at least 2 weeks in advance

### Communication Standards
- Respond to Slack messages within 4 business hours
- Email response SLA is 24 hours
- Use async communication by default; reserve meetings for decisions

## Performance Reviews

Performance reviews occur bi-annually (June and December). You will:
1. Complete a self-assessment
2. Receive peer feedback (minimum 3 reviewers)
3. Have a 1:1 review with your manager
4. Receive compensation review results within 2 weeks of review
